GAME GUIDE

HOW TO PLAY ?

  1. MAKE TWO TEAMS

    Pick how many players are on each team. Pick how many turns each team gets.

  2. SEE WHO GOES FIRST

    Tap each team’s button to get a number. The team with the bigger number guesses first.

  3. CHOOSE A CHECKER

    Pick someone on the actor’s team to hold the phone. That’s the checker. Show the card only to the actor. Say the category out loud, like “Animals.” Keep the word a secret.

  4. ACT IT OUT

    Tap Start Game to see the word. Take your time choosing a card and planning your act. When the actor is ready, tap Start Timer. The actor stays quiet and acts while the other team guesses.

  5. GUESS ONE WORD IN TWO MINUTES

    The team can keep guessing the same word until time runs out. Tap Lock Screen to hide the card. Listen for the countdown in the last five seconds.

  6. SCORE AND SWITCH

    Checker, tap Correct when the team guesses the word. They earn 1 point and their turn ends immediately. If time runs out, they earn 0. The other team guesses next.

  7. WIN THE GAME!

    Both teams get the same number of turns. The team with the most points wins.

WAYS TO ANSWER

Actor, try these moves when you hear a guess.

↕ YES / OO

Nod your head up and down.

↔ NO / HINDI

Shake your head left and right.

🙌 MAYBE / PWEDE

Lift both hands up to your shoulders.

Actor, stay silent. Don’t mouth the word or draw letters in the air. Keep the card hidden from the guessers.
